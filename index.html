How We Work

Our Project
Process

A structured, transparent approach from first contact to final handover.

01

Free Site Visit

Our expert team visits your site at no cost to assess conditions, dimensions, and load requirements.

02

Requirement Analysis

We analyze load-bearing needs, IS code requirements, and recommend the optimal flooring system for your use case.

03

Quotation

Transparent, itemized quotation with no hidden costs. We present multiple options to match your budget and quality expectations.

04

Execution

Skilled crew mobilizes with all machinery — laser screed, power trowels, concrete pumps — for seamless, high-speed execution.

05

Final Finishing

Power trowel finishing, curing compound application, and quality checks to achieve the specified flatness and surface hardness.

06

Delivery & Handover

Final inspection walkthrough with the client, documentation, and post-project support for any touch-ups needed.
